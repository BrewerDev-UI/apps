You

Little kids

In the car seat: no puffy coats under the straps. Dress kids in thin warm layers, buckle snug, then put the coat or a blanket on top. That's the American Academy of Pediatrics' advice.

Young kids lose heat faster, so they get one more layer than you. Check fingers, ears and cheeks often when it feels like 20°F or colder.
