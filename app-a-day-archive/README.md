# App a Day archive

The first two App a Day apps, kept here as plain copies.

- `thanksgiving-game-plan/` — App #1, Nov 3, 2026. Turkey sizing, thaw dates, hour-by-hour plan, menu, shopping list, who-brings-what.
- `morning-after-26/` — App #2, Nov 4, 2026. 2026 midterm results guide: scoreboard, Senate races, power check, Missouri, what's next.

Stacked Sky (repo root) and Blend Lab (`blend-lab/`) have their own phone versions in this repo.

Each folder's `index.html` is the whole app. Open it in a browser to use it.
