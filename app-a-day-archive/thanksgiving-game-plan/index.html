      Tricks for this year's table

      The vinegar move

      • Whole Foods named vinegar a top food trend for 2026. A small splash wakes up rich, heavy dishes.
      • Whisk a teaspoon of cider or red wine vinegar into gravy to cut the grease.
      • Add a splash of cider vinegar to the stock before it hits the stuffing.
      • Taste rich dishes at the end and add acid before more salt.

      Carve it in six moves

      1. Rest the bird at least 30 minutes so the juices settle.
      2. Slice through the skin between leg and body, then pop the thigh joint out.
      3. Split each leg into drumstick and thigh at the joint.
      4. Cut along the breastbone and pull each breast off whole.
      5. Slice the breasts crosswise, about half an inch thick.
      6. Twist off the wings and save the frame for stock.

      Food safety basics

      • Thaw in the fridge, about one day for every 4 to 5 pounds.
      • The turkey is done at 165°F in the thickest part of the breast and thigh.
      • Get leftovers into the fridge within 2 hours and eat them within 3 to 4 days.
      • Smoking? Smaller birds, about 14 pounds or less, get through the danger zone faster.
